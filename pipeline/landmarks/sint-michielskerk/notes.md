## What it looks like

Jesuit church (1607-1611, Jan Persijn), late-Gothic, north-south oriented three-aisled basilica of red-brown brick with stone plinth and trim; big pointed window in the brick south front dated 1610. Two five-storey towers flank the choir on the north side, with constricted slate needle spires. Slate roofs.
