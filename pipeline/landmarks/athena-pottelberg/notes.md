# Athena campus Pottelberg

Priority: **P3** (Pottelberg 4, secondary school; tile `-2_2`). Not a heritage landmark: fixed because it is the biggest building in its tile and the render got it badly wrong.

- Name from Nominatim (not in our OSM extract). GRB: 1746889 (3D GRB HN_P99 26.85 m)

## What it looks like

A U of long school wings (about 10 m wide, 5-6 storeys) around a courtyard with low buildings, red tiled pitched roofs (orthophoto). Wall colour not checked (no Street View); brick assumed.

## Render fix

**Done (piece 9, 2026-11-13):** `massing.json`. The render had one flat 27 m block with orange walls. Now: wings to 14 m (GRB footprint) with gabled roofs from DHMV II: west wing ridge 21.5 m, north wing 27 m (west half) and 17.5 m (east half), east wing 23 m and 18.5 m.
