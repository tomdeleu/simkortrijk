# Damiaankerk (former Sint-Jozefkerk of the Karmelieten)

Priority: **P2**

- OSM: way 568089136 (`building=church`, disused place of worship, Q104684557), Aalbeeksesteenweg 7
- GRB: 1739208 (48.6 x 27.4 m, 3D GRB 16.6 m)

## What it looks like

Brick church of the Carmelite convent: dark brown brick, steep red pantile roofs, a big west gable on the Aalbeeksesteenweg with a round rose window, a short round turret with a conical roof at the south-west corner. The Karmelietenklooster (`../karmelietenklooster/`) lies against its south side.
