# Verrijzeniskerk

Priority: **P3** (Burgemeester Pyckestraat, Doorniksewijk; tile `0_2`)

## What it looks like

Modern round parish church: low white walls, a pleated roof of green copper star points rising to a central green copper lantern with a thin spire and a cross. In the orthophoto the roof is a green disc with radial folds.

## Render fix

**Done (piece 8, 2026-11-13):** `massing.json`. The render had a flat grey disc. Now: white wall at 4.5 m (GRB footprint), a 16-sided green copper cone to 11.5 m, a lantern to 15 m and a spire to 23 m. The pleats themselves are left to the painter.
