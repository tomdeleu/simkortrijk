- Onroerend Erfgoed: 60201 (`heritage=4`)

## What it looks like

Large neo-Romanesque/neo-Gothic cruciform church in buff yellow stone with dark slate roofs. A crossing tower with a clock and belfry stage carries an octagonal slate needle with four small corner turrets. The west front and both transept fronts have big rose windows, red doors and slender corner turrets with slate cones; the choir ends in an apse to the east. It stands in a small green square with trees.
