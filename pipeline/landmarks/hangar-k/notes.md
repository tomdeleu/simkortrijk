## What it looks like

Former railway hangar, now a start-up campus. Orange-red brick walls, and along both long sides a row of 16 brick gable ends: the roof is 16 red bays side by side, each with a glazed strip, ridges across the hall. A big "HANGAR K" sign on the long front facing the square.
