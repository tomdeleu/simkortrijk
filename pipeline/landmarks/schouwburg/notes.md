## What it looks like

Neo-Flemish-Renaissance theatre (1909-1911, J.R. Vanhoenacker) on the Schouwburgplein: red brick with white stone bands, stepped and curved gables, corner turrets with slate spires, steep slate roofs, glass dome inside.
