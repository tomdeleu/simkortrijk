# K in Kortrijk + Sint-Janstoren

Priority: **P2**

- OSM / ids: K in Kortrijk way 1014884611 (Q2398510); Sint-Janstoren way 566720379 (46 m, Q2329309)
- GRB / render: K: OIDN 4600431 (11 157 m², 26.6 m); Sint-Janstoren: OIDN 363006 (41.8 m)

## What it looks like

Shopping centre (2010, Robbrecht en Daem): a huge flat block clad in dark natural stone (#4C4D50) with a flat dark roof and a large steel-and-glass **hip** roof (about 112 × 31 m, light blue-grey, a flat light strip on top) over the inner square. The orthophotos (refs 07/08) show a hip, not a barrel. Sint-Janstoren: 11-storey residential tower, dark stone plinth, white cladding above (#EEEDE8), trees on the roof.

## Prompt line (draft)

K in Kortrijk: a large dark-stone shopping block with a long glass barrel roof; next to it a white residential tower with trees on top.
