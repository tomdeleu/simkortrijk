## What it looks like

Early-Gothic collegiate church (1199-1211), cross basilica of dark grey Tournai limestone (#8C8C88, cooler and darker than Sint-Maarten), some sandstone and brick. Two square west towers with stepped buttresses and round stair turrets, under LOW slate tent (pyramid) roofs (no spires; cross on the north tower, weathercock on the south). Nave, transept and big chapels under slate saddle roofs, aisles under lean-to roofs, choir with flying buttresses. The Gravenkapel on the south side: Sainte-Chapelle-like, tall pointed windows, balustrade with pinnacles, small stone ridge turret.

## Prompt line (draft)

Onze-Lieve-Vrouwekerk: dark grey stone Gothic church with two squat square west towers under low slate pyramid roofs (no spires), steep slate nave and transept roofs, flying buttresses at the choir.
