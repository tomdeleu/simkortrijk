## What it looks like

Enclosed 17th-century beguinage (0.7 ha): about 41 small two-storey brick houses, WHITEWASHED (#F2EFE8) with black-tarred plinths, under red/orange clay pantile saddle roofs (#B8573A), some slate. Small walled front gardens with round-arched gates. Irregular cobbled square with one big old tree in the middle and the statue of Johanna of Constantinople; stepped gable of the Grootjuffrouw house (1649) and the octagonal stair tower of the Sint-Annazaal.
