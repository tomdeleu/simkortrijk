# Buda-eiland: Budatoren, Budafabriek, Budabrug

Priority: **P2**

- OSM / ids: Budatoren way 579199788; Budafabriek way 196220409 (arts centre); Budabrug way 567809512 (Q2053179, lifting); Oranjerie way 1211323591
- GRB / render: Budatoren OIDN 1744135 (291 m², 27 m); Budafabriek OIDN 5823260 (1 796 m², 15.5 m)

## What it looks like

Budatoren: former Tack brewery tower wrapped in a glass skin; the old brick silhouette shows through (pale blue-green glass over brick, ~27 m). Budafabriek: converted textile mill, low brick sheds with a brick street pavilion (51N4E) and a roof terrace. Budabrug: low steel lifting bridge (ZJA), grey steel with a curved white/grey canopy-like truss.

## Prompt line (draft)

Buda island: a tall square brick tower sheathed in pale glass, low brick factory halls with saw-tooth roofs, and a low grey steel bridge.
