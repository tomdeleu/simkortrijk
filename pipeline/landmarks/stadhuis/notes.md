## What it looks like

Long late-Gothic/Renaissance façade on the north-west side of the Grote Markt: eleven bays, two storeys of white natural stone (#E6E1D6) on a plinth, statues of the counts of Flanders in niches, corbelled corner and middle turrets, a parapet with gargoyles. Steep slate saddle roof with nine wooden dormers and a slender slate ridge turret (dakruiter). The complex runs back around two courtyards in brick.
