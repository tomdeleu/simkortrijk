## What it looks like

Neo-Gothic parish church, 1863-1870, west tower 1875. Dark red-brown brick, buttresses, tall lancet windows with tracery, a dark slate roof that covers nave and aisles in one span, and a choir with a three-sided apse to the east. The west front on the Pijlstraat has a red door, a rose window and the tower: a square brick shaft with a clock and a belfry stage, then an octagonal slate needle with four small corner pinnacles. The south side faces the paved Sint-Rochus parking (red paving and trees; the render shows a construction site from the old ground data).
