# K Tower

Priority: **P2** (Diksmuidekaai 11, by the Collegebrug; tile `-1_-2`, windows `-2_-2` / `-2_-3`)

- GRB 6802479 (ground floor, 181 m²) + GBA 701163 (upper floors, 294 m²). Not in our OSM extract (Google Maps: "K Toren").

## What it looks like

Residential tower by Philippe Samyn and Partners, completed 2018: 66 m, 20 floors, white staggered glass and steel volumes with white balconies, on columns at the ground. It replaced the 60 m Collegetoren of the Sint-Amandscollege. Sources: [Visit Kortrijk](https://visitkortrijk.be/en/k-tower), [Infosteel](https://www.infosteel.be/en/steel-construction-project/4139-mirrored-patchwork-for-delicate-tower), [Samyn and Partners](https://samynandpartners.com/portfolio/reconstruction-of-the-k-tower/).
