## What it looks like

Free-standing square belfry in the middle of the Grote Markt (remnant of the 1411 cloth hall), slightly leaning. Red-brown brick (#9A4A35) with white natural-stone quoins, string courses and a stone ground storey with a pointed gate. Five storeys under a constricted (ingesnoerde) octagonal slate needle spire with sound openings, crowned by a gilded Mercury weathervane; four corner turrets with their own small constricted slate spires; two gilded jacquemarts "Manten en Kalle" on the front. WWI memorial at its south-east side. Around it is open cobbled square.

## Prompt line (draft)

Belfort on the Grote Markt: a slender free-standing square red-brick tower with pale stone corners, a central dark slate needle spire and four small slate corner spires, gilded figure on top, standing alone on the open square.
