# Sint-Amandscollege (now Guldensporencollege campus Kaai)

Priority: **P2** (Diksmuidekaai / Kollegestraat; tiles `-2_-3` / `-2_-2`)

- OSM school area way 124535851. GRB 1730972 (glass school on the quay), and the old college wings.

## What it looks like

The old college: neo-Gothic red-brown brick with white stone bands, dark slate roofs, a chapel with a Gothic gable, wings around an inner garden. On the Diksmuidekaai: a long modernist school block of 3-4 storeys with dark glass curtain walls in a dark grey frame, flat roof, glass entrance box. The white K Tower (`../k-tower/`) stands next to it.
