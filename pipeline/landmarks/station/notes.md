## What it looks like

Post-war station (1951-1956, Pierre Pauwels): long symmetrical building of yellow-beige brick (#D3BF92) with blue hardstone (#5E6468) plinth, cornice and window frames, a raised central hall with tall windows; flat roofs. On the track side a long concrete platform canopy, partly glazed. In front the Stationsplein.

## Prompt line (draft)

Station: a long symmetrical yellow-beige brick building with a taller central hall and flat roofs, long concrete canopies over the tracks.
