# Spinnerij De Stoop (lofts)

Priority: **P2** (Spinnerijkaai 43-45, on the Kanaal Bossuit-Kortrijk; tile `3_-3`, windows `3_-4` `3_-3` `4_-4` `4_-3`)

- GRB 1734211 (3560 m², 14.25 m). Neighbours fixed in `../spinnerijkaai/`.

## What it looks like

Former textile mill of Camiel De Stoop, now lofts and offices: red-brown brick, four storeys with tall windows, flat dark roof, a square brick tower with an open belvedere at the west end of the canal front, a crenellated parapet at the east end. Next to it on the quay a low yellow building with a columned front, further east modern white apartments.
